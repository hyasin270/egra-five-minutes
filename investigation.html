The ask (operator, 15 Sep 2026)

Replace the enumerator-run EGRA/EGMA battery (English, Urdu, maths) with a coach-administered version: the coach records the child, models pre-score, the coach corrects on a pre-filled form (same HITL pattern as NIETE /observe), both AI and coach values plus the audio are stored. Target 5 min per child, 5 children in 30 min. Pops up after an observation visit. Deliverable: a plan with flows, model per stage, and a roadmap to production.

Headline findings

  1. Baseline 21 min/child → target 5 min is a 3.6–4.2× compression; it has to come from removing item-by-item tapping, stop rules, and a deliberate subset (60-s reads kept for benchmark comparability).
  2. The field team rejected the WhatsApp ASER tool on 11 Sep (one prompt per item, 10–15 min/child, no offline, accent worries). Manual/override modes shipped since; the per-item shape and online-only did not.
  3. Probes: a Pro-class audio LLM segments and labels a whole session and audits protocol (caught a slip the reviewer missed) but its timestamps drift by ~1 min; Soniox timestamps the 60-s window exactly and diarises child vs enumerator on 8 kHz audio. Rule: STT cuts, LLM labels.
  4. No commercial Urdu phoneme API exists (Azure PA: 33 locales, no ur). Urdu = STT + forced aligner + per-phone GOP, calibrated on the 187 coach-reviewed clips. English = Azure / SpeechAce.
  5. Zero new tables for v1: aser_sessions + reading_assessments + numeracy_assessments already hold the AI-vs-coach pair, who-scored, audio, transcript, word timestamps and per-word errors.
  6. NIETE observe is two recordings (classroom + debrief), not three; best hook is the classroom-recording-received point; observation completion must stay independent.
