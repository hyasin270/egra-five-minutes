EGRA/EGMA staging build: plan conformance and coach experience

Review lens: product lead with EGRA/EGMA background, checking the build against our plan and against the coach's day. Reviewed: 27 Sep 2026 · bead bd-ak1go · read-only. What was built: the frozen snapshot in ../src/ (8 commits, 783116cc → e81dbc03). File references below are relative to ../src/. The plan it should serve: ../../PLAN.html (sections named by their ids, e.g. #battery), ../../meetings/2026-09-15_alignment-call.md, ../../harness/HARNESS_RESULTS.md §8, ../../research/04_niete_observe_hitl.md, ../../harness/flow/egra-review-flow.json, ../../BASELINE.md. Live evidence, also read-only: the shared Supabase schema and rows, queried through the analyst role, and the staging bot's logs in Axiom (digital-coach-logs, service digital-coach-staging, 17 to 24 Sep). The exact queries are in the appendix.

The short version

  1. This build puts the May 2026 enumerator test on WhatsApp almost in full. It does not build the plan's five-minute test. It keeps 14 of the 17 May subtests. Those 14 took an enumerator about 14 minutes of a 21-minute session. On WhatsApp my estimate is about 22 minutes per child (range 18 to 25) and about 1 hour 50 minutes for five children. The target is 5 minutes and 30 minutes, so the build runs 3 to 4 times over and saves no time against today's enumerator.
  2. The subtests are close to the reverse of the plan's list. The build keeps what the plan drops: listening comprehension, the 100-letter grids, the 50-word list and patterns. It leaves out what the plan keeps: made-up words, first sounds, reading numbers aloud, one-minute quick sums, and the photo of written sums. The Urdu and English stories and their comprehension questions are the only overlap.
  3. The coach marks every item, one WhatsApp message per item. That is 64 Correct/Incorrect taps plus 10 typed answers per child. On 11 Sep the field team rejected exactly this one-question-per-message design, and PLAN.html#today says the plan removes it. The AI does nothing. The new table even has a database rule (a check constraint) that stops it from ever storing an AI score.
  4. Audio is recorded for the listening-comprehension questions only, the one subtest the plan drops, and only after the child has already answered. The timed reading minutes, which the evaluation showed the AI can count to within 5 words, are never recorded.
  5. Things are broken on staging right now, and TESTING.md does not say so. Every stimulus picture sits on a private storage address that returns an error. All 3 live attempts to send the passage picture failed (19, 21 and 24 Sep). The letter, word and story grids use the same address, so the coach would get a "Ready? ▶️ Start" button with nothing to show the child. - The listening-comprehension Flow is registered on staging. - The database columns that would hold the voice note were never added. So if a coach finishes that subtest with a voice note, saving the row fails and the coach's own marks are lost.
  6. No child has been tested yet. Across 9 staging sessions: - 1 subtest was ever saved. - 0 voice notes were captured. - 0 sessions finished. - The Flow was "completed" 10 to 15 seconds after the child was picked. That is a tester tapping through; a real passage read twice would take far longer.
  7. Children are only partly random. The coach picks the class, and each tap draws one child with replacement. Across five draws from a class of 9 there is a 74% chance of drawing someone twice. Cancelling and re-drawing is a way to choose the child. The child's database ID is never saved, so repeat tests of the same child cannot be linked, and linking them over time is the whole point of testing often.
  8. Some pieces are worth keeping: - the switch-plus-region gate - the real item bank with answer keys - stimulus pictures drawn by code, not by AI, with a correct Nastaliq font - picking the child at random on the server from the real roster - uploading audio to our own storage straight away - the careful routing and "never hijack" checks - the chat-loop fix (bd-eg7st)

These are good parts to build the plan's version from.

The build's reason for dropping made-up words is SpeechAce's poor automatic scoring (egra-egma-real-items.json:6-20). That reason does not apply here, because nothing is scored automatically, and TESTING.md says so at :172-178. 3b. Are the timed subtests really timed?

  • Who runs the clock: the coach taps ▶️ Start and ⏹ Stop in the chat. The server measures the time between the two taps arriving (handler.js:573-588), so WhatsApp's own delay on each tap is included.
  • The 60-second limit is not enforced. There is no countdown and no stop alert (TESTING.md:201-206). The only instruction ("please self-time this") rode on the picture caption, which fails today.
  • One phone cannot do both jobs. The same phone shows the child the letters and holds the Start and Stop buttons. Once Start is tapped, the "Timer running" message pushes the picture up the chat. The coach then has to:
  • scroll back and hand over or hold up the phone,
  • keep time without a clock,
  • remember which of up to 100 items were wrong,
  • take the phone back to tap Stop and type the item numbers.

Real EGRA avoids this with a printed card for the child. The plan does the same.

3c. Stop rules

  • Discontinue marks all of the first row wrong (handler.js:590-601). The Urdu word list's own rule is "0 to 1 correct" (egra-egma-real-items.json, familiar_word_reading.urd.discontinue_rule), so a child who read 1 word is recorded as 0.
  • The button also appears on both stories, which have no stop rule in the item bank.
  • Reading comprehension is never gated on the story. gatedOnGrid is defined in item-bank.service.js:65,69 and used nowhere. The data note says so at egra-egma-real-items.json:473.

In May, only 1,382 of 2,233 children (62%) have a timing for the Urdu story, and 952 (43%) for the English one (baseline_subtask_timings.csv). The May form gated these with no_orf and continue_rc (BASELINE.md:36). Here, every child is asked all 12 comprehension questions, including children who read nothing of the story. - Maths laddering and the "continue" gates are not built (TESTING.md:228-232). - None of the plan's stop rules exist: the fall-back branch, "questions only if a line was read", and "4 wrong in a row stops maths".

3d. Language and order

  • The order is right: Urdu, then English, then maths, following the real instrument and the call.
  • Within the Urdu block the build starts with listening comprehension, then the 100-letter grid. The plan would start with the story.
  • Test content is in its real language. Coach instructions are in English everywhere, including inside the Flow.

3e. What the child sees versus what the coach sees

  • The letter, word and story grids are drawn by code from the exact item text, which is the right way to do it. But they are the enumerator's sheet, with "1.", "2.", … in every cell (grid-image.service.js:63-68), and the child is shown them.
  • The stories are chopped into numbered cells, one word per cell. Punctuation gets its own numbered cell. The English story has 8 such cells (items 7, 14, 23, 36, 41, 54, 62, 68), for example "7. ." A child reading from that is not reading connected text.
  • Scoring counts those cells as words: a perfect English read scores 68, not 60, which is 13% too high (item-bank.service.js:50; tokens 68 against 60 words).
  • The result is labelled "items/min" (handler.js:396), which cannot be compared with the 60-to-90 WCPM benchmark the plan relies on.
  • The Urdu story has punctuation joined to the words, so it is not affected.
  • Listening comprehension: the Flow tells the coach not to show the screen to the child (Flow JSON :51), which is correct. But the passage text starts with "(FOR ENUMERATOR: READ THE FOLLOWING PASSAGE)", shown as-is.
  • Maths: items go only to the coach's phone as text. Nothing says whether to read "Choose the larger number: 3826 or 2863" aloud or show it. That choice changes the skill being tested (reading numerals against listening), and different coaches will choose differently.
  • The coach never sees an answer key. correct_answer_note is in the item bank for every item, but no code sends it (handler.js:223-230 sends only item.prompt). So for "Why did Sara run home happily?" each coach decides for themselves what counts as right.
  • Missing maths pictures. For represent_1a ("How many bananas are there?"), TESTING.md says the coach is shown a description of 6 bananas (:193-198). The code does not send it, so the coach sees only the question.
  • Typos in coach-facing text: "On what did did Sara wake up early?" and "WHo walked with Sara" (egra-egma-real-items.json:565,575). The Flow's example text has the corrected wording.

4. Coach experience

Copy and language. - Every coach-facing line is English and hard-coded: prompts, buttons, Flow screens and the end summary (TESTING.md:216-220). - There is no preferred_language, no string catalog, and no Urdu. Our checking-form draft and the ICT observation form are in Urdu. - Some wording is internal or confusing for a coach: - "Discontinue rule: If the child scores 0/10 correct on the first row…" appears in the caption. - The Flow says "WhatsApp will ask you to record…"; it is Rumi that asks, not WhatsApp. - "items/min" is jargon.

Clarity. - There is no overall progress. The coach sees "Item 3/8" but never "subtest 6 of 14", or any sign of how much of a roughly 20-minute session is left. - The 5 grid subtests depend on a caption for their name and instructions. With the picture failing, the coach sees only "Ready?" and a Start button.

Number of taps. 83 taps, 11 typed answers and 2 voice notes per child (§2). That is about ten times the plan's journey, and it is the pattern the field team rejected on 11 Sep.

Recovering from mistakes. - There is no undo. One tap on Correct or Incorrect records the mark and moves on. - Buttons carry no item number (handler.js:205-212), and any Correct/Incorrect/Pass tap is accepted while marking is open (handler.js:603-607). So a double tap, or a tap on an older item's button further up the chat, is recorded against whichever item is current. With 64 item messages in the chat, this will happen. - /cancel only works in button-driven steps. Typed as the child's name, it becomes the name (handler.js:506-511). While typing wrong items or the last item it gets "not a number" (:513-538). TESTING.md:69 promises "/cancel at any point". - The voice-note step has no skip. Anything typed there gets "Please use the buttons above" (handler.js:539-546), but that step has no buttons. The only way out is a voice note or cancelling the whole child. - Cancelling loses the child's progress. If the child is absent or the recording fails, the coach can only cancel the whole session, which throws away the on-screen summary, and draw again. - The access gate fails silently. A coach without the right region who types /egra falls into general chat. Commit 3c024544 (bd-eg7st) traces the 18 Sep "send me the story" loop to exactly this, and 7 /egra messages on staging that day started no session. The fix sends such coaches to /reading; it does not tell them the test is not switched on for them. - The session expires 1 hour after the last step saved (session-state.service.js:21).

What the coach sees at the end. - One plain text list per subtest, for example "• Letter Identification (Urdu): 43/47 correct (41.3/min)", plus "no AI … is used" (handler.js:328-343). It arrives only if all 14 subtests are finished. - There is no level, no benchmark and no TIP group (D6). - There is nothing for the teacher and no class view. - There is no portal page. - There is no "child 2 of 5" and no list of who is left today.

How it is started. By typing /egra or four other phrases (handler.js:64-68), not offered after the observation. That is fine for roadmap step 2.

The 11 Sep field team said something relevant here: in NIETE, ASER is run separately from observations (meetings/2026-09-11_ASER-roll-out.md:40). The plan's "offer after the lesson" (#journey) conflicts with that feedback. This is for the plan owner to settle, not a fault in this build.

5. Strategic read

This is a different path from the plan, not an early step along it. Phase 0 of the plan would test the risky parts: - one recording per block, taken while the child reads from a printed card - the per-child checking form - the storage shape that keeps the AI's answer and the coach's answer side by side - the 5-minute clock

This build tests something else: a WhatsApp chat version of the full 21-minute enumerator battery. The differences are built in, not surface-level:
