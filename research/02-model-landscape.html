02 — Model landscape for AI-scored EGRA/EGMA (English + Urdu + spoken math), Sep 2026

Written: 2026-09-15 · Scope: which speech / phoneme / audio-LLM / vision models can pre-score a coach-recorded, ~5-minute child battery (ages 6–10, Pakistani government schools) for a human-in-the-loop coach. Method: vendor docs, model cards and papers fetched 2026-09-15; every claim carries a URL. Anything not confirmed against a primary page is marked unverified. Companion docs: ../BASELINE.md (enumerator timings, 8 kHz SurveyCTO audio), ../probes/.

Headline: English has three mature scripted pronunciation stacks (Azure PA, SpeechAce, Language Confidence) with phoneme scores and kids evidence. Urdu has zero commercial phoneme-scoring APIs (Azure PA supports hi-IN and ta-IN but not ur-; SpeechAce/ELSA/SpeechSuper/Language Confidence/iFlytek are English-or-8-language only). Urdu therefore rides on (a) commercial ASR with word timestamps (Soniox, Deepgram, Scribe, AssemblyAI Universal-2, Speechmatics, Whisper) + edit-distance alignment — our existing FluencyService pattern — and (b) open-weight phone recognisers / forced aligners that do cover Urdu (ZIPA, MMS_FA, IndicMFA, mms-1b-all) for letter-sound and phoneme-awareness items. Audio-native LLMs are useful as verbalisers* of externally computed evidence, not as standalone per-word scorers: the 2026 literature documents prior-over-evidence diagnosis, ASR that silently corrects children's errors, and leniency on low-quality speech.

A.2 What this means for us

  1. English: Azure PA gives everything the EGRA English block needs (per-word omission/insertion/mispronunciation, phoneme accuracy, NBest "sounded-like" in en-US, timestamps) but only within 30 s per call with miscues on. A 60-s ORF passage forces continuous mode and our own reference-vs-hypothesis diff (which FluencyService already does). SpeechAce is the only vendor with published child-speech accuracy and explicit phonics/nonword support (custom pronunciations), at ~2× Azure's price and with a 45-s Pro cap.
  2. Urdu: no vendor. The nearest Azure locale is hi-IN, whose phoneme inventory overlaps Urdu heavily (Hindustani), but using hi-IN with Devanagari reference text for Urdu children is an un-validated hack — transliteration errors would be scored as child errors. Not recommended without an eval.
  3. Kiswahili (for TZ later): likewise absent from every PA vendor; Azure STT has sw-KE/sw-TZ (§C).

Child-speech accuracy for these on Urdu: none published. The closest data points are (i) the ASER dataset — 5,301 Indian children aged 6–14, 81,330 clips in Hindi/Marathi/English, ASR-based reading-level classifier 86% accurate for English (arXiv 1912.04381); (ii) EGRA-Xhosa (letters + words, wav2vec2/HuBERT/Whisper; performance dominated by training-data balance) (arXiv 2505.17371); (iii) Bambara children's reading ASR — WER 0.42→0.22 after adaptation, under-10s remain the main error source (arXiv 2606.31508); (iv) BabAR phoneme recognition → developmental measures for young children (arXiv 2603.05213). No Urdu children's speech corpus exists publicly: CLE's corpora are adult (ELRA S0403), UrduSpeech (156 h, 1,000+ speakers, 2026) is adult (arXiv 2605.17846). Our 207 SurveyCTO recordings + 187 coach reviews are therefore a genuinely scarce asset — but at 8 kHz/12 kbps AAC they are below every model's 16 kHz expectation (see BASELINE.md).

CPU vs GPU: MFA/IndicMFA and 64 M ZIPA are CPU-feasible; MMS-1B, XLS-R-300m phone models and WhisperX are practical on CPU only at low throughput (a 60-s clip in tens of seconds — unverified; benchmark on the Railway worker before committing). A single T4/L4 on Modal or an EC2 g5 covers the fleet.

Take-aways. (1) Word-timestamped Urdu ASR is cheap and plentiful (Soniox, Deepgram, Scribe, AssemblyAI-U2, Speechmatics, whisper-1) — $0.10–0.36/hr. (2) The big 2026 flagships have dropped Urdu from their newest tiers (AssemblyAI 3.x, Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, Qwen3-ASR, Voxtral, Parakeet/Canary) — pin to Soniox/Deepgram/U-2 and keep a Whisper/seamless fallback. (3) No provider publishes Urdu child-speech numbers; on English children, zero-shot Whisper-class WER is 10–15% and can be ~9% with 10–50 h of child fine-tuning (Kid-Whisper), but fine-tunes generalise poorly across child datasets (MyST → CMU Kids WER rises) (CHSER). (4) Dysfluent/hesitant reading is where strong ASRs hurt most: they auto-correct the child's errors ("stronger ASR models tend to correct pronunciation errors during transcription", smaller models detect more) (arXiv 2601.14744) — a direct threat to correct/incorrect-per-word scoring, and an argument for phone-level (§B) rather than word-level evidence on the decoding subtasks.

Published evals and failure modes that matter for us

  • Prior over evidence. Across three audio-capable LLMs (incl. Gemini 3.0 Flash, GPT-4o-audio, Qwen3.5-omni) on L2-ARCTIC, 39.6% of judged cells contained coherent reasoning supporting a wrong rating vs 15.8% supporting a right one; phoneme feedback collapsed to a fixed "hard-phones" inventory regardless of L1 or whether audio was even supplied. Conclusion: LLMs are "verbalisers of externally computed pronunciation evidence" not diagnostic engines (arXiv 2606.15325).
  • ASR rectifies errors. Cascaded ASR+LLM MDD gets recall 17.8% with strong ASR; instruction-tuning lifts F1 to 62.8 (arXiv 2601.14744). Canonical-prior bias is now a named problem in MDD ("explicit canonical priors bias predictions toward intended targets"; prompt-free CTC model reaches F1 71.8 on L2-ARCTIC) (arXiv 2604.22133).
  • Direct audio-LLM scoring of children underperforms a structured pipeline. On Tamil/Malay children, fine-tuned ASR → objective metrics (PER, WER, speech rate, pause ratio) → GPT classifier "achieves significantly higher accuracy than multimodal GPT" fed the raw audio (arXiv 2505.19671). K-Function (phoneme WFST + LLM grading) reports PER 1.39% on MyST and close alignment with human proctors on ORF-style tasks (arXiv 2507.03043).
  • Zero-shot speech LLM scoring on speechocean762 (half children): Qwen2-Audio leniency on low scores (SLaTE 2025); text-only "Read to Hear"/TextPA with gpt-4o-mini / gemini-2.0-flash is competitive and complementary out-of-domain (EMNLP 2025); fine-tuned speech-LLM reaches SOTA phoneme-accuracy PCC 0.743 but trails Azure PA on prosody/total (arXiv 2509.02915); rubric-guided fine-tuning for multi-rater reading assessment (arXiv 2603.16889).
  • Implication: use an LLM (text) as the final aggregator/explainer over (ASR words + timestamps + phone-level GOP + reference alignment), never as the primary per-word judge. If we do send audio to an LLM, send the segment with the reference and ask for a structured diff, and measure it against coach corrections before trusting it.

Realistic targets: for passage ORF (English, regional accent, zero-shot Whisper-class), expect WER 10–15% and WCPM correlation ≥0.95 with humans — good enough for coach pre-fill. For isolated items (letters, nonwords, single words) word-level agreement drops to κ 0.2–0.4 unless the recogniser is constrained to the item set (phone-level or grammar-constrained decoding). Urdu / Hindi children: no published word-level agreement anywhere — we will have to produce it from our own 187 coach-reviewed EGRA recordings.
